“The Guy Who Started With an Idea”

There was a guy named Maya. He didn’t have everything figured out in life. Sometimes he had big dreams, sometimes random ideas, and sometimes he just sat there wondering what he should do next.

But one thing was different about him — whenever he got an idea, he didn’t just want to imagine it. He wanted to make it real.

One day, he started working on something small. It wasn't perfect. He didn't know everything. He made mistakes, changed his plans, and started again.

Some days, nothing seemed to work.

But Maya kept going.

Slowly, the small things he created started becoming something bigger. And one day, he realized that he had changed too.

He was no longer the guy waiting for the right moment.

He had become the guy who created his own moment.

Because sometimes, you don't need to know the whole path. You just need the courage to take the first step
