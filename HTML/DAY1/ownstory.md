Maya, this is my own story.

When I was studying in 10th grade, it was during the Corona period. We had a lot of holidays, so I used to spend my time going around my hometown and playing games like kabaddi and cricket with my friends.

At that time, I only had a Samsung 3G phone. I mostly used that phone just to play Free Fire. One day, I went to a function, and I noticed the cameraman taking photos and videos. Later, when I saw the photos and videos he had created, I really liked them. That was the moment I started becoming interested in photo and video editing.

But I only had a 3G phone, so editing was really difficult. I couldn't record proper videos, the quality wasn't good, and even when I tried editing a video in CapCut, downloading or exporting it was a problem. Because of all these difficulties, I eventually stopped editing.

But my interest in editing never really disappeared.

I really wanted to buy a better phone—something with a good camera, good quality, and enough storage. So after some time, I bought a Samsung A14 5G. With that phone, I slowly started improving my editing skills and learning new things.

I started making small edits for my friends and family just for fun. Then I got an idea: maybe I could actually earn some money through editing. But I also felt that I needed more experience if I wanted to take it seriously.

So I worked for six months and saved money to buy an iPhone 14 Pro. After buying it, I started taking editing more seriously. One day, I got an editing opportunity from a coffee shop and earned ₹500 from it. After that, I slowly started charging small amounts like ₹50 or ₹100 for the edits I made.

I started using that money for my daily expenses. It may not be a huge amount, but for me, every ₹50 or ₹100 means something because I earned it through something I genuinely love doing.

And even now, I don't want to stop here.

I want to learn more, improve my skills, do bigger projects, and take editing much more seriously. This is how my interest in editing slowly became a part of my life.
